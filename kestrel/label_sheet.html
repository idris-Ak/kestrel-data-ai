How to label (open/close)

For each comment: pick the overall sentiment, then mark each theme the customer has an opinion about as − (negative) or + (positive). Leave other themes blank. Judge meaning, not keywords: sarcasm is negative. Ignore placeholders like [NAME]. Use Other when an opinion fits no theme. Tick Personal info visible if the text still identifies someone (a name, phone, plate). Work at a natural pace; labels autosave in this browser.
